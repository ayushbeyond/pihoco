## 1. The one-line story

**"Find the money on your menu in 60 seconds. Free."**

Supporting line: *A menu X-ray from a menu engineer, not a chatbot. Every finding shows its evidence, and your sales numbers never leave your browser.*

### What it is, in plain words
A free web tool. A restaurant or café owner pastes, uploads or photographs their menu. In about a minute they get:
1. A score out of 100 across five parts of the menu.
2. The three fixes worth the most money, with the evidence behind each.
3. A money estimate they can adjust (if they add covers and average bill).
4. Better descriptions for their dearest dishes.
5. How their prices compare with local menus (Dubai today) and with official restaurant inflation (UK, US).

### Who it's for
| Audience | What they care about | Lead with |
|---|---|---|
| Independent café owners (Dubai first) | Coffee prices, footfall, rent | "Is your flat white priced right? Dubai median is AED 25." |
| Independent restaurant owners | Margins, food cost, staff | "Three fixes, ranked by money." |
| Cloud kitchens / delivery brands | Commission, menu size | "Too many items? We count them for you." |
| Restaurant groups / consultants | Many menus, quick diagnosis | "X-ray a menu before the first meeting." |

### Why it's different (say these, they're true)
- **Rules, not AI.** No AI model reads the menu. Every point comes from published menu-engineering rules, and the evidence is shown under each score.
- **Private by design.** Covers and average bill are used for maths in the browser and never stored unless the owner chooses to share them.
- **Real local prices.** The Dubai index is built from menus on venues' own websites, with the source kept for every price.
- **A person behind it.** The full scorecard is reviewed by Piyush, a menu engineer, within 48 hours.

## 2. Proof points: what we can and can't claim

### ✅ Claims that are true today (10 Nov 2026)
- "Built on **560 menu prices** from **14 public Dubai menus**, collected November 2026."
- "**22 dishes** with prices from at least 5 Dubai venues."
- "Restaurant inflation data from the **UK ONS** and **US BLS**."
- "Your sales numbers never leave your browser."
- "Free for a limited time."

The homepage and app count these numbers live from the database, so they grow automatically. Re-check them before reusing them in a static post.

### ❌ Never claim (until it's true and documented)
- "Thousands of restaurants" / "all of Dubai" / "industry benchmark".
- "AI-powered" (it isn't, and that's a selling point).
- Any comparison for Indian, Lebanese or other restaurant dishes. Fewer than 5 menus cover each dish so far.
- Revenue results ("owners made +20%"). There are no client outcomes yet. Use "scenario", never "forecast".
- Testimonials or review counts. The site's testimonial block stays hidden until real ones exist.

## 3. Content pillars

1. **Price Index drops** (weekly): one item, one chart, one question. Built from the table above.
2. **Menu teardowns** (weekly): X-ray a public menu (with permission, or anonymised), show the score and three fixes. *Never shame a named venue without consent.*
3. **Menu psychology in 30 seconds** (2× week): one rule per post, the same rules the X-ray uses.
4. **Behind the method** (fortnightly): how the X-ray decides, which builds trust in "rules, not AI".
5. **Owner math** (fortnightly): covers × bill × lever, worked through with round numbers.

## 4. Ready-to-post content

### 4.1 Instagram / LinkedIn carousels (5–7 slides each)

**C1. "What Dubai charges for coffee, Nov 2026"**
1. What Dubai cafés charge for coffee. 12 venues. Real menus.
2. Cappuccino: AED 25 median (typical 20–26).
3. Flat white: AED 25 · Latte: AED 25 · Americano: AED 20.
4. Spanish latte: AED 28. The condensed-milk premium is about AED 3.
5. Matcha latte: AED 29, the priciest everyday drink.
6. Where does yours sit? Free 60-second Menu X-ray (link in bio).
Caption: *From public menus on venues' own websites, Nov 2026. A PIHOCO dataset, not an industry survey.*

**C2. "Your menu is a list. It should be a ladder."**
1. Most menus are lists. Good menus are ladders.
2. A flat section (every dish ±10%) gives guests no reason to trade up.
3. Add one premium anchor about 30–40% above your top dish.
4. Don't list cheapest to dearest: guests shop by price.
5. The X-ray checks every section's ladder in seconds. Free.

**C3. "The easiest money on a menu"**
1. The easiest money on your menu isn't a price rise.
2. It's the add-on line: extra shot, add cheese, add a side.
3. 600 covers a week × 4–10% take-up × AED 10 = AED 1,000–2,600 a month.
4. That's a scenario, not a promise. Your numbers decide.
5. The X-ray finds whether you have one, and prices it from your covers.

**C4. "Why we don't use AI to read your menu"**
1. Every menu tool says "AI-powered". We don't.
2. AI guesses. Rules show their working.
3. Each point in your score has the evidence under it.
4. Your covers and bill stay in your browser. Nothing trains a model.
5. Built by a menu engineer. Free for a limited time.

**C5. "Your prices are older than you think"**
1. When did you last change your prices?
2. UK hotels, cafés & restaurants: +3.8% in the 12 months to Sep 2026 (ONS).
3. US food away from home: +3.5% in 12 months (BLS, Sep 2026).
4. Not repricing = a quiet price cut every month.
5. The X-ray shows how far behind you are (UK/US today, UAE soon).

**C6. "Five words that sell a dish"**
1. "Chicken curry" vs "Chicken curry, slow-cooked in a smoky tomato masala".
2. Add one taste word: crisp, smoky, silky.
3. One method: charred, slow-cooked, wood-fired.
4. One origin or house detail: house-made, family recipe.
5. 10–20 words, on your top 8 earners only.
6. The X-ray rewrites three of your descriptions for free.

### 4.5 Email sequence after a free X-ray (lead gate)

**Day 0, "Your Menu X-ray: {score}/100"**
Score, top 3 fixes, link to shared result. "Piyush will send your full scorecard within 48 hours."

**Day 2, "The full scorecard for {restaurant}"** (manual, Piyush)
PDF attached. One paragraph on the biggest opportunity. Founding-rate offer for the audit.

**Day 5, "One fix you can do tonight"**
Their highest-confidence non-price fix (e.g., mark 2–3 chef's picks). No pitch.

**Day 10, "Founding rate: {X} of 5 spots left"**
What the audit adds (recipe costs, sales mix, contribution). Credit the scorecard fee if paid. Book-a-call link.

**Day 30, "Re-X-ray your menu"**
"Changed anything? Run it again and compare." Keeps them in the loop for the paid tier.

## 5. Pricing plan

### Phase 0: Launch (now → 31 January 2027, or the first 100 full scorecards)
Everything free, to build volume, data and stories.
- Free X-ray, free shareable result, free Price Check.
- Free full PDF scorecard, reviewed by Piyush (cap at the first 100 to protect his time).
- Message: *"Free for a limited time. Founding owners keep free re-checks for life."*

### Phase 1: After launch (from 1 February 2027)

| Tier | Price | What's in it | Why it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Menu X-ray** | Free, always | Score, 3 fixes, rewrites, price check, share link | Top of funnel; every run adds data |
| **Full Scorecard** | USD 49 · AED 179 · GBP 39 · SGD 65 | 2-page PDF, every fix ranked and priced, reviewed by Piyush in 48h | Small "yes"; **100% credited** against the audit |
| **Price Watch** | USD 19/mo · AED 69/mo · GBP 15/mo | Monthly re-X-ray, local index alerts ("cappuccino median moved"), inflation drift alert, 3 competitor menus tracked | Recurring revenue once a city has 50+ menus |
| **Menu Profit Boost Audit** | USD 750 founding (5 spots) → USD 1,200 | Recipe costs, sales mix, contribution, 10-page audit, 1 review call | Core consulting offer (existing site pricing) |
| **Menu Engineering Sprint** | USD 2,500–3,800 | Audit + redesign + reprice + staff brief | Existing site pricing |
| **Group / Consultant** | USD 99/mo, up to 10 menus | Bulk X-rays, branded PDF exports | Later, once Price Watch is proven |

AED / GBP / SGD audit and sprint prices follow `assets/js/config.js` on the site.

### Why this structure
- **Free stays free**, so the funnel never closes and the data keeps growing.
- **USD 49 filters tyre-kickers**, but it's fully credited, so it never feels like a cost on the way to the audit.
- **Price Watch only launches when the data deserves it.** Don't sell a subscription to a 14-menu index. The gate is 50+ Dubai menus and monthly refreshes.
- **Founding scarcity is real.** Five audit spots, publicly counted.

### What to measure (weekly)
- X-rays run (live counter in the database)
- Result → lead conversion (target 15–25%)
- Lead → paid scorecard after launch (target 10%)
- Scorecard → audit (target 20%)
- Menus added to the index (target +10 a month)

## 7. Brand rules for all content
- Fonts: Newsreader (headlines, brass italics on one word) + Instrument Sans.
- Colours: ivory #f3eee6, ink #1c1915, night #171411, brass #946f3e, gain #1E3027, leak #A65E49.
- Money is always a **range**, labelled **scenario**.
- Data always carries its **source line and month**.
- Never name and shame a venue. Anonymise or get consent.
- British spelling. Short sentences. One idea per slide.
