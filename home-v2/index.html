Questions

Before you ask.

Do you need access to our POS?

No. Item-level sales exports for 8 to 13 weeks are enough. We never need guest or card data.

We don't have clean cost data. Can we start?

Yes. We build the costings with your head chef and you keep them. Without sales exports, we can run a lighter audit from your menu and a sample week.

What happens to our data?

It's covered by an NDA, used only for your engagement and deleted 90 days after it ends. Nothing is published without your written approval. Privacy.

Do you work outside the UAE?

Yes. Audits run remotely, so we work with restaurants and cafés in the UAE, UK and Singapore, and elsewhere by arrangement.
