Questions

Before you ask.

Do you need access to our POS?

No. We work from exports; we do not need a live POS login, guest records or payment-card data. Ideally, share 8–13 weeks of item-level sales by outlet and channel.

We don't have clean cost data. Can we start?

We can help rebuild recipe costings from recipe specifications, portion quantities, yields and current supplier prices. If item-level sales data is missing, we can still do a menu and pricing review, but we cannot produce a reliable popularity-versus-contribution matrix or call it a full menu engineering audit.

Can you calculate actual profitability from a menu photo?

No. A menu photo can support a preliminary review of structure, descriptions and published prices. Verified dish-level contribution requires item-level sales, recipe quantities and yields, and current ingredient costs. We distinguish estimates from calculations based on your operating data.

Do you guarantee a specific profit increase?

No. We identify and prioritise evidence-based opportunities; actual results depend on execution, customer response, purchasing, operations and other factors. We do not promise a fixed revenue or profit uplift.

What happens to our data?

Your project data is used only for the engagement and handled under the agreed confidentiality terms. The retention period and deletion process follow our Privacy Policy and any signed NDA. Nothing is published without your written approval.

Do you work outside the UAE?

Dubai and the UAE are our primary market. We take selected UK and Singapore engagements remotely; local price benchmarking is only included where we have enough relevant, current comparison data. Other markets are assessed case by case.
