Questions

Before you ask.

Do you need access to our POS?

No. We work from exports: 8–13 weeks of item-level sales. No POS login, guest records or card data.

We don't have clean cost data. Can we start?

Yes. We can rebuild recipe costings with your chef from portions, yields and supplier prices. Without item-level sales we can still review the menu and pricing, but not build a full matrix.

Can you calculate actual profitability from a menu photo?

No. A photo supports a first look at layout, descriptions and prices. Real dish earnings need your sales, recipes and ingredient costs, and we always say which is which.

Do you guarantee a specific profit increase?

No. We find and rank the opportunities your data supports. Results depend on how changes are made and how guests respond, so we never promise a fixed uplift.

What happens to our data?

It is used only for your project, under the agreed confidentiality terms, our Privacy Policy and any NDA. Nothing is published without your written approval.

Do you work outside the UAE?

Dubai and the UAE come first. We take selected UK and Singapore projects remotely. Local price comparisons are included only where we have enough current data.
