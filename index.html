      What does it cost?

      The Menu Scorecard is free. The Menu Profit Boost Audit has a founding rate of USD 750 for our first five clients, and the five-week sprint USD 2,500. Multi-site groups are quoted on request. See all prices.

      Do you work with restaurants outside India?

      Yes. Audits run remotely on a shared folder and two or three video calls, so we work with independent restaurants and cafés in the UAE, UK and Singapore, and elsewhere by arrangement. We invoice in USD, and can quote in AED, GBP or SGD.

      Do you need access to our POS?

      No. Standard item-level sales exports for 8 to 13 weeks are enough. We never need guest or card data.

      We don’t have clean sales or cost data. Can we start?

      Yes. We build the costings with your head chef, and you keep them. If sales exports aren’t available, we can run a lighter audit from your menu and a sample week of sales.

      What happens to our data?

      Your sales and cost data are covered by an NDA, used only for your engagement and deleted 90 days after it ends. We never publish results without your written approval. Read more.
