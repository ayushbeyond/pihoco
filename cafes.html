Where café margin hides

  1. 01 · Drinks

    The size ladder and the milk fridge

    Most of the till is coffee, so small gaps repeat hundreds of times a week.

    • Does the large earn more cash than the regular, or just cost more?
    • Do oat milk, extra shots and syrups cover what they cost?
    • Are signature drinks earning more than a flat white, or just taking longer?

  2. 02 · Bakery counter

    What sells out, and what gets binned

    Bakes look profitable on paper until you count what is left at close.

    • Sell-through by item and by day of the week
    • Par levels: how many of each to bake
    • Counter order: what guests see first

  3. 03 · Add-ons & pairings

    The second item

    How often a coffee leaves with something to eat is one of the biggest levers a café has.

    • Attach rate: food with drinks, by daypart
    • Breakfast and lunch bundles that lift the bill without cutting margin
    • One line staff can say at the till

  4. 04 · Delivery & takeaway

    Same drink, different cost

    Commission, cups, lids and bags turn a good dine-in price into a thin delivery one.

    • A separate delivery price list
    • Packaging cost per item
    • Which items travel badly and should come off the app
