    How it works

    The method, in plain words.

    Steps

    1. Fill in the engagement and the trading days.
    2. Upload menu, sales and cost CSVs (or one combined file, or start from the owner's Menu X-ray).
    3. Set VAT, commission and the target cost %.
    4. Run the analysis. Read the Data checks on the overview first.
    5. Review the matrix by section, then the action plan.
    6. Add your judgement in Consultant review.
    7. Open the report and save it as PDF. Download a backup.

    The maths

    Net price = sales ÷ units, with VAT removed when prices include it.

    Contribution per dish = net price − direct cost − commission.

    Popularity line = 70% of the average units per dish in the section (Kasavana & Smith).

    Contribution line = the section's sales-weighted average contribution per dish.

    Star above both · Plowhorse popular, earns less · Puzzle earns more, sells less · Dog below both.

    Not classified: dishes without a cost ("Needs costing") or without sales.

    Opportunities: plowhorse = price test × units; puzzle = extra units to the popularity line × contribution per dish. Monthly, constant volume, scenarios only.
