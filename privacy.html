Privacy & data

Your menu and numbers stay yours.

What we collect, why, where it is kept and when we delete it, in plain words.

On this website

  • Contact form. Your name, email, restaurant and message are emailed to us through Web3Forms so we can reply. We use them only to answer you and follow up about our services.
  • Anonymous click counts. We count page visits and which buttons are clicked (for example "free menu check"), with the page and the referring website. We do not record names, emails, IP addresses or device IDs, and we set no cookies.
  • Your currency choice is remembered in your own browser only.
  • No advertising trackers, and we never sell or share your details.

In the free Menu X-ray

  • Your menu and result. The dishes, prices, restaurant name, city and score are saved so your share link works.
  • Covers and average bill are used only for the maths in your browser. They are not stored unless you choose to include them when you contact us.
  • If you leave your details (name, email and optionally WhatsApp), they are saved and emailed to us so Piyush can follow up.
  • Price Check. When you compare competitors' public menu prices, those public prices are added anonymously to our local price index unless you untick the box. Your own sales and costs are never added.

During a paid engagement

  • We ask only for standard exports: item-level sales, recipe cards, supplier prices. We never need POS logins, guest data or card data.
  • Your data is used only for your engagement, under the agreed confidentiality terms and any NDA we sign.
  • It is deleted 90 days after the engagement ends, unless you ask us in writing to keep it for follow-up work.
  • We never publish your name, results or figures without your written approval. Case studies are anonymised unless you agree otherwise.
  • We do not combine one client's data with another's, and we do not sell data.
