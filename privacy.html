Privacy & data

Your menu and numbers stay yours.

Restaurants trust us with their sales and costs. This page says plainly what we collect, why, and when we delete it.

What this website collects

  • What you type into a form: name, email, business, city and any message.
  • A menu file or link, if you request a free Menu Scorecard.
  • Your answers and score, if you ask us to email your 60-second menu check.

Form details are stored in our Google Workspace (Sheets and Drive). We use them only to reply, prepare your scorecard and follow up about our services. We don’t sell or share them, and we don’t use advertising trackers on this site.

Client data during an engagement

  • We ask only for standard exports: item-level sales, recipe costs or supplier invoices. We never need POS logins, guest data or card data.
  • Your data is covered by a non-disclosure agreement signed before we start.
  • It is used only for your engagement and deleted 90 days after the engagement ends, unless you ask us to keep it for follow-up work.
  • We never publish your name, results or figures without your written approval. Case studies are anonymised unless you agree otherwise.

We don’t pool or sell data

We don’t combine one client’s data with another’s to build industry datasets, and we don’t sell data. Any benchmarks we quote come from published sources.

Your choices

Ask us at any time to see, correct or delete what we hold about you: raaikwaarpiyush@gmail.com. We reply within five working days.
