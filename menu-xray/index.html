Questions

Before you upload.

Is it really free?

Yes. The Menu X-ray is free and stays free. You only pay if you choose the full audit, where we work with your real recipe costs and sales.

What do I need to upload?

Just your menu, as a PDF, a photo or pasted text. Covers and average bill are optional and stay in your browser.

Does AI make up the findings?

No. Your menu is read, you check every dish, and the findings come from published menu-engineering rules and real Dubai menu prices. Each finding shows the rule and the evidence behind it.

Where do the price comparisons come from?

From menus Dubai restaurants and cafés publish on their own websites, refreshed regularly. Where we don't have enough local prices for a dish, we say so instead of guessing.

Is the money figure a promise?

No. It's a scenario based on your menu and simple, stated assumptions, so you can see which fix is worth doing first. You can change the assumptions in your result.

I'm not in Dubai. Does it work?

The menu score and the fixes work for any menu. The price comparison is strongest in Dubai today, with more cities coming.
